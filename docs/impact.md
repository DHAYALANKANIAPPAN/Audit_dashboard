# AuditPulse 360 - Operational Impact & Effort Reduction Model

## Baseline Assumptions (Pre-Digitalization)
Manual quality audit processes in a medium-sized manufacturing plant (approx. 5 lines, 3 shifts):
1. **Audit Schedule Planning & Roster Coordination:** ~6 hours/week
2. **Shop-Floor Paper Checklist Execution & Manual Transcription:** ~14 hours/week
3. **NC Follow-Up, Email Chasing & Escalation:** ~10 hours/week
4. **CAPA Verification & Paper-based Head Sign-off:** ~5 hours/week
5. **Weekly & Monthly Compliance KPI Compilation (Excel / PPT):** ~8 hours/week
**Total Manual Overhead:** ~43 hours/week

## Post-Digitalization (AuditPulse 360)
1. **Bulk CSV Plan Upload & Auto Roster Engine:** ~0.5 hours/week (91% reduction)
2. **PWA Mobile Inspection with Auto-NC Raising:** ~4 hours/week (71% reduction)
3. **Automated Reminders & Background Escalations (BullMQ):** ~0.5 hours/week (95% reduction)
4. **Digital Two-Tier Approvals with Evidence Viewer:** ~1.5 hours/week (70% reduction)
5. **Real-time Analytics Dashboard & 1-Click ISO/IATF Export:** ~0.5 hours/week (93% reduction)
**Total Overhead with AuditPulse 360:** ~7 hours/week

**Net Plant Time Saved:** ~36 hours/week (~83% reduction in administrative overhead)
